# 㽲痛切痛同附陰陽易病辨誤（1851、1849 年篇末署記，京都大學 テ/7） / Kōtsū settsū dō, with In’yō ekibyō bengo [Abdominal-pain terminology and textual notes]: Transcription Dataset

《㽲痛切痛同》及附篇《陰陽易病辨誤》是多紀元佶的江戶時代漢文醫籍考證。首篇比較《金匱要略》《千金方》等所載㽲痛、切痛、絞痛的字義。附篇對讀古本，追查《傷寒論》陰陽易病名的用字。兩篇末各有辛亥（1851）及己酉（1849）署記，京都大學富士川文庫所藏冊末另有朱筆題記。

Kōtsū settsū dō and the appended In’yō ekibyō bengo are two Literary Chinese philological essays by Taki Genkitsu. The first compares historical expressions for abdominal pain. The second compares the wording of disease names across medical witnesses, including editions of the Shanghan lun. The essays bear internal dates corresponding to 1851 and 1849. This Fujikawa Collection copy also contains a vermilion inscription.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。㽲、㽱、絞等字形照底本。原刻小註分列，朱題及淡印保持可定位。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Variants such as 㽲, 㽱 and 絞 follow the printed witness. Small annotations and the vermilion inscription are indexed separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
